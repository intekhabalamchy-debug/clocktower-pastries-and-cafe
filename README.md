# 🥐 Clocktower Pastries & Cafe (時計塔のパン屋と喫茶)

> *"May all your bacon burn! ...but our sourdough rises to golden perfection upon my fire."*

An enchanted, Studio Ghibli-inspired digital dining and bakery menu based on **Howl's Moving Castle** and **Calcifer's hearth**. Featuring stone-baked loaves, warm elixirs, and whimsical confections.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🔥 Calcifer's Hearth Showcase:** Thematic split-screen hero section with live oven status indicators and glowing ember animations.
- **✨ Starry Night & Rising Embers Backdrop:** Ambient CSS-animated night sky with twinkling stars and drifting hearth sparks.
- **📜 2x2 Grand Menu Grid:**
  - **Hot & Cozy Drinks:** Calcifer's Hearth Spiced Cocoa, Forest Spirit Matcha Latte, Clocktower Chai, Sootsprite Sesame Milk.
  - **Refreshing Elixirs:** Meadow Honey Lemonade, Sea Breeze Cream Soda, Orchard Blossom Iced Tea, Strawberry Fizz.
  - **Sweet Pastries & Desserts:** Red Bean Brioche Bun, Howl's Berry Tart, Sootsprite Chocolate Choux, Honey Castella Cake.
  - **Breads & Hearth Bakes:** Soot-Scorched Sourdough, Rosemary Focaccia, Brioche Rolls, Seeded Boule.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Clean layout across mobile, tablet, and widescreen displays.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** – Semantic markup and accessible typography
- **CSS3** – CSS Grid (2x2 layout), Flexbox, dot leader pricing, and keyframe animations
- **Font Awesome 6** – Thematic icons
